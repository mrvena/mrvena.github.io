Key Vocabulary Terms

rounding: Changing numbers to nearby tens or hundreds to estimate quickly.
estimate: Finding a close, reasonable answer before doing exact math.
multiply: Adding equal groups repeatedly.
add: Combining partial product amounts into a final total.
partial product: The product obtained when multiplying a factor by one digit of another factor.
regroup: Carrying a value (10 ones to 1 ten) into the next place-value column.
Section 2 • 5 Minutes Instruction

Objective 1: Multiply Two 2-Digit Numbers (No Regrouping)

Example: 32 x 12 = 384
Step 1: Estimate First

Use rounding to estimate: Round 32 → 30 and 12 → 10.
30 x 10 = 300. Our answer should be close to 300.

Step 2: First Partial Product (Ones Place)

multiply 2 (ones digit) by 32:
2 x 32 = 64 (First partial product).

Step 3: Second Partial Product (Tens Place with Zero Placeholder)

Place a 0 in the ones column, then multiply 1 (tens digit) by 32:
10 x 32 = 320 (Second partial product).

Step 4: Add Partial Products

add 64 + 320 = 384. (Close to estimate 300!)

Error to Avoid: Forgetting the Zero Placeholder!

When you multiply by the 1 in 12, you are multiplying by 10 (1 ten). Always put a 0 in the ones place before multiplying by the tens digit!

Section 4 • 5 Minutes Instruction

Objective 2: Multiply Multi-Digit Numbers with Regrouping

Example: 375 x 25 = 9,375
Step 1: Estimate First

Round 375 → 400 and 25 → 30.
400 x 30 = 12,000. Our answer should be close to 12,000!

1
Step 2: Ones Step Regrouping (Orange Row)

• 5 x 5 = 25 (write 5 in O, regroup 2 to T)
• 5 x 7 = 35 + 2 = 37 (write 7 in T, regroup 3 to H)
• 5 x 3 = 15 + 3 = 18 (write 18 in Th & H)
First Partial Product = 1,875

2
Step 3: Tens Step Regrouping (Blue Row)

Put 0 in ones column. Multiply by 2 tens:
• 2 x 5 = 10 (write 0 in T, regroup 1 to H)
• 2 x 7 = 14 + 1 = 15 (write 5 in H, regroup 1 to Th)
• 2 x 3 = 6 + 1 = 7 (write 7 in Th)
Second Partial Product = 7,500

Step 4: Add Partial Products

1,875 + 7,500 = 9,375.

Critical Error: Multiply FIRST, then ADD Regrouped Digits!

Never add the carried number before multiplying! Always calculate top digit x bottom digit FIRST, then add the regrouped number to that product.

Section 6

Key Takeaways & Turn In

1. Estimate First

Round factors to make sure your final product is reasonable!

2. Zero Placeholder

Always put a 0 in the ones place when multiplying by the tens digit.

3. Multiply Then Add

Multiply top digit by bottom digit FIRST, then add carried numbers!

4. Combine Sums

Add both partial product rows carefully to reach your final answer.
