Key Vocabulary Terms

unit fraction: A fraction with a numerator of 1, such as 1/4, 1/6, or 1/8.
quotient: The result or answer obtained when one number is divided by another.
dividend: The total amount or starting quantity being divided into smaller parts.
divisor: The size of each equal group or fractional piece you are dividing by.
fraction model: A visual bar or diagram showing division into equal fractional parts.
equal parts: Sections of a whole that are all identical in size and fraction measure.
Section 2 • 5 Minutes Direct Instruction

Objective 1: Meaning of Division by a Fraction (Lesson 8-3)

Example: 5 ÷ 5/8 = 8
Step 1: Understand the Division Question

Rylee has a 5-inch block of tofu. She wants 5/8-inch pieces.
Ask: "How many 5/8s are in 5?"

Step 2: Divide Each Whole into Eighths

Each 1-inch block contains 8 eighths (1/8).
5 whole inches equal 5 x 8 = 40 eighths.

Step 3: Count Groups of 5 Eighths

Group 40 eighths into sections of 5 eighths: 40 ÷ 5 = 8.
So, 5 ÷ 5/8 = 8 pieces of tofu!

Error to Avoid: Confusing Total Parts with Section Size!

When dividing a whole number by a fraction smaller than 1, your quotient will be GREATER than the starting whole number because you are making smaller parts!

Section 6

Key Takeaways & Turn In

1. Meaning of Division

Division by a fraction asks: "How many copies of the divisor fit inside the dividend?"

2. Visual Models

Draw bars or number lines to visualize breaking wholes into fractional units.

3. Common Denominators

Convert fractions to equal denominators (e.g. 3/4 = 6/8) before counting groups.

4. Larger Quotients

Dividing a positive quantity by a fraction less than 1 results in a larger whole count!
