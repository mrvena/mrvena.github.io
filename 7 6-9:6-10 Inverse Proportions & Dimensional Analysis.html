Section 1

Why We Need This Math & Key Vocabulary

Road Trips

If you drive faster, it takes less time to reach your destination. Speed and travel time are inversely proportional!

Team Work

If more people help clean a park, the total hours it takes to finish decreases. This is an inverse proportion.

Cooking & Baking

Recipes might call for 32 fluid ounces, but your measuring tools use cups. Dimensional analysis helps you convert units easily.

International Travel

Different countries use different currencies. We use conversion factors to safely exchange our dollars into Euros or Yen!

Key Vocabulary Terms

inverse proportion: When an increase in one quantity causes a proportional decrease in another.
reciprocal factor: The flipped fraction of a number; used to represent the opposite change in an inverse proportion.
dimensional analysis: The process of converting from one unit system to another using fractions.
conversion factor: A unit ratio that compares two equal amounts in different units (like 12 inches / 1 foot).
unit ratio: A ratio where the denominator is 1, often used to establish rates.
exchange rate: The specific conversion factor used when swapping one currency for another.
Section 2 • 5 Minutes Direct Instruction

Objective 1: Inverse Proportions

Inverse Rule: Value 1 × Value 2 = Constant
Example Problem Statement:

A ranch has enough food to feed 5 horses for exactly 12 days. If the ranch buys 5 more horses (giving them 10 horses total), how many days will the same amount of food last?

Step 1: Identify the Inverse Relationship

Problem: 5 horses eat for 12 days. How long for 10 horses?

More horses means the food will run out faster! As the number of horses increases, the number of days decreases. This is an inverse proportion.

Step 2: Solve with Multiplication

Because it is inverse, the total amount of "horse-days" of food remains constant.
• 5 horses × 12 days = 60 total horse-days of food.
• Set up the new equation: 10 horses × (d days) = 60.
• 60 ÷ 10 = 6. The food will last for 6 days!

Error to Avoid: Setting it up like a Direct Proportion!

Never divide the two starting numbers! If you set up an equation like 5/12 = 10/x, you will get the wrong answer because one side is going up while the other side is going down! Always multiply the starting values together first.

Section 4 • 5 Minutes Direct Instruction

Objective 2: Dimensional Analysis

Conversion Factor: 1 lb = 16 oz
Example Problem Statement:

A newborn zoo animal weighs exactly 120 ounces. Using the conversion factor (1 pound = 16 ounces), find the animal's weight in pounds using dimensional analysis fractions.

Step 1: Set Up the Units to Cancel

Problem: Convert 120 oz to lb.

Start by writing your original measurement as a fraction over 1: (120 oz / 1).

Step 2: Multiply by the Unit Ratio

• Because "ounces" is in the numerator, you must multiply by a fraction with "ounces" in the denominator to cross them out.
• Multiply: (120 oz / 1) × (1 lb / 16 oz).
• The 'oz' units cross out, leaving 120/16 lbs.
• 120 ÷ 16 = 7.5 pounds!

Unit Fraction Canceling

1 lb / 16 oz
Final Math (120 ÷ 16): 7.5 lbs

Error to Avoid: Multiplying by the Wrong Factor!

Always make sure the unit you are trying to GET RID OF is placed diagonally across in your multiplication (one on top, one on bottom) so they can cleanly cancel each other out!

Section 6

Key Takeaways & Save / Turn In

1. Inverse Nature

In an inverse proportion, when one number goes up (like speed), the other number must go down (like time)!

2. The Constant

Multiply the two starting numbers together in an inverse proportion to find the constant amount of work or distance.

3. Unit Ratios

A conversion factor is just a fraction that equals 1, like (12 inches / 1 foot), used to swap between different measurement sizes.

4. Canceling Units

In dimensional analysis, make sure the unit you want to get rid of is on the opposite side of the fraction so it perfectly crosses out.
