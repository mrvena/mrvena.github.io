Why We Need This Math & Key Vocabulary

Aquarium pH Levels

Aquarium fish require water pH p where 6.5 < p < 7.5 (a conjunction AND)!

Speed Limits

Safe driving speed s must satisfy 45 <= s <= 65 mph on highways.

Manufacturing Tolerance

A part width w must satisfy absolute tolerance |w - 50| <= 0.5 mm!

Target Heart Rate

Exercise heart rate h stays in zone when |h - 140| <= 20 bpm!

Key Vocabulary Terms

compound inequality: Two inequalities joined by the word AND or OR.
conjunction: A compound inequality joined by AND (intersection where both parts are true).
disjunction: A compound inequality joined by OR (union where at least one part is true).
boundary point: The starting or ending value of a region on a number line.
absolute value: The distance of a number from 0 on a number line (|x| >= 0).
interval notation: A shorthand way to write solution sets using brackets [ ] and parentheses ( ).
Section 2 • 5 Minutes Instruction

Objective 1: Write & Solve Compound Inequalities (AND / OR)

Example: -7 <= 2x - 1 < 3
Step 1: Identify Conjunction (AND) vs Disjunction (OR)

• conjunction (AND): Solution is the intersection (overlapping region).
• disjunction (OR): Solution is the union (all shaded regions combined).

Step 2: Add or Subtract to Isolate Variable Term

Add 1 to all three parts of -7 <= 2x - 1 < 3:
-7 + 1 <= 2x < 3 + 1 → -6 <= 2x < 4.

Step 3: Divide by Coefficient

Divide all parts by 2:
-6 / 2 <= 2x / 2 < 4 / 2 → -3 <= x < 2.

Step 4: Express Solution Set & Graph

Set-builder: {x | -3 <= x < 2} | Interval: [-3, 2).
Closed circle at -3, open circle at 2, shade in between!

Error to Avoid: Reversing Inequality Signs!

Whenever you multiply or divide all parts of an inequality by a negative number, you MUST flip the direction of the inequality symbols (e.g., < becomes >)!

Section 4 • 5 Minutes Instruction

Objective 2: Solve Absolute-Value Inequalities (|x| < a vs |x| > a)

Example: |x| + 4 < 14 → -10 < x < 10
Step 1: Isolate the Absolute Value Expression

Subtract 4 from both sides of |x| + 4 < 14:
|x| < 10.

1
Step 2: "Less Than" (< or <=) = Conjunction (AND)

• |x| < a means distance from 0 is less than a units.
• Rewrite as: -a < x < a (x is between -a and a).
• Example: |x| < 10 → -10 < x < 10.

2
Step 3: "Greater Than" (> or >=) = Disjunction (OR)

• |x| > a means distance from 0 is greater than a units.
• Rewrite as: x < -a OR x > a.
• Example: |x| > 10 → x < -10 OR x > 10.

Step 4: Solve & Graph Solution Set

Set-builder: {x | -10 < x < 10} | Interval: (-10, 10).

Key Principles for Absolute Value
Rule 1: Less Than (< or <=)
|Expression| < a
→ -a < Expression < a (Conjunction AND)
Rule 2: Greater Than (> or >=)
|Expression| > a
→ Expression < -a OR Expression > a (Disjunction OR)

Critical Error: Always Isolate Absolute Value First!

Never remove absolute value bars until all outside addition, subtraction, multiplication, or division is simplified first!

Section 6

Key Takeaways & Turn In

1. Isolate First

Always isolate the absolute value expression before splitting into two inequalities!

2. Less Than = AND

|x| < a translates to -a < x < a (intersection region in between endpoints).

3. Greater Than = OR

|x| > a translates to x < -a OR x > a (disjunction pointing outwards).

4. Flip on Negative Division

Multiplying or dividing by a negative number flips the inequality symbol direction.
