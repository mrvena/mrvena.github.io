Today's Learning Objective

I can model percents, convert between percents, ratios, fractions, and decimals, and compare them accurately.

Section 1

Why We Need This Math & Key Vocabulary

Wildlife & Nature

Example: Biologists count 100 sea turtle eggs in a beach nest and find 62 hatchlings. Expressing 62 out of 100 as 62% helps track survival rates.

Test & Quiz Scores

Example: Getting 21 out of 25 questions right on a math quiz converts to 84%, making it easy to compare scores across different tests.

Collections & Inventory

Example: If 40% of a coin collection is quarters and 35% is dimes, subtracting from 100% shows 25% (or 1/4) are nickels.

Comparing Deals

Example: Store A offers 3/8 off a jacket, while Store B offers 40% off. Renaming both to percents (37.5% vs. 40%) reveals the better discount.

Key Vocabulary Terms

percent (%): A ratio or comparison of a quantity to 100, meaning "per hundred" (n/100 = n%).
equivalent ratio: Ratios that express the same relationship between numbers, such as 3/20 and 15/100 (15%).
simplest form: A fraction or ratio where the numerator and denominator have a greatest common factor (GCF) of 1.
proportion: An equation stating that two ratios are equal, such as x/100 = 3/8, used to solve for percents.
repeating decimal: A decimal with one or more digits that repeat endlessly, shown with a bar (e.g., 1/3 = 33.3% with a bar over 3).
like form: Renaming fractions, decimals, and percents into the same format so they can be compared accurately.
Section 2 • 5 Minutes Direct Instruction

Objective 1: Modeling Percents & Converting Ratios

Core Rule: part / whole = n / 100 = n%
Example Problem Statements:

(A) Out of 100 sea turtle eggs in a nest, 62 produce hatchlings. What percent of the eggs produce hatchlings? (B) Rename 9/10, 43/1000, and 17/25 as percents. (C) Write 35% and 16.4% as ratios in simplest form.

Step 1: Ratios to Percents (Denominators of 10, 100, 1000, or Factors of 100)

Problem: Express 62 out of 100, 9/10, 43/1000, and 17/25 as percents.

• 62 out of 100: 62/100 = 62% (already per hundred).
• 9/10: Multiply top and bottom by 10 to get 90/100 = 90%.
• 43/1000: Divide top and bottom by 10 to get 4.3/100 = 4.3%.
• 17/25: 25 is a factor of 100 (25 × 4 = 100). Multiply top and bottom by 4 to get 68/100 = 68%.

Step 2: Renaming Percents as Ratios in Simplest Form

Problem: Write 35% and 16.4% as ratios in simplest form.

• 35%: Write as 35/100. Divide both by their GCF (5): (35 ÷ 5)/(100 ÷ 5) = 7/20.
• 16.4%: Write as 16.4/100. Multiply top and bottom by 10 to clear the decimal: 164/1000. Divide by GCF (4): (164 ÷ 4)/(1000 ÷ 4) = 41/250.

Error to Avoid: Forgetting to Scale to 100 or Leave Decimals in Fractions!

A fraction like 7/10 is NOT 7%—the denominator must be 100 first (70/100 = 70%). Also, when turning a decimal percent like 16.4% into a ratio, never leave a decimal inside the numerator of your final fraction!

Section 4 • 5 Minutes Direct Instruction

Objective 2: Fractions, Decimals, Percents & Comparing

Key Strategy: Convert to Like Form
Example Problem Statements:

(A) In Leo's coin jar, 35% are quarters, 40% are dimes, and the rest are nickels. Write the percent of nickels as a simplified fraction and a decimal. (B) Rename 0.7429 as a percent rounded to the nearest tenth. (C) Rename 5/8 as a percent using division and 1/6 as a percent using a proportion. (D) Compare 0.06/0.24 and 25%.

Step 1: Percents ↔ Decimals & Remaining Parts

• Finding the rest: 35% + 40% = 75%, and 100% - 75% = 25% nickels.
• Percent to Fraction & Decimal: 25% = 25/100 = 1/4. Move the decimal point 2 places left (÷ 100): 25% = 0.25.
• Decimal to Percent: Move the decimal point 2 places right (× 100): 0.7429 → 74.29% ≈ 74.3%.

Step 2: Fractions to Percents (Two Methods) & Comparing

• Method 1 (Division): For 5/8, divide 5 ÷ 8 = 0.625 → move decimal 2 places right = 62.5% (or 62 1/2%).
• Method 2 (Proportion): For 1/6, set up n/100 = 1/6 → 6n = 100 → n = 16.66... → 16 2/3% (or 16.6̄%).
• Comparing in Like Form: For 0.06/0.24 vs. 25%, divide 0.06 ÷ 0.24 = 0.25 = 25%. So 0.06/0.24 = 25%.

Decimal Point Shift Rule
Decimal → Percent (× 100) 0.7429 ➔ 74.29% ≈ 74.3%
Move decimal 2 places RIGHT
Percent → Decimal (÷ 100) 25% ➔ 0.25
Move decimal 2 places LEFT
Like Form Check: 0.06/0.24 = 0.25 = 25%

Error to Avoid: Moving the Decimal Point the Wrong Way!

When changing a decimal to a percent, move the decimal 2 places to the RIGHT (0.4 becomes 40%, NOT 4%). When changing a percent to a decimal, move 2 places to the LEFT (8.5% becomes 0.085, NOT 0.85).

Section 6

Key Takeaways & Save / Turn In

1. Per Hundred

A percent compares a number to 100. Scale ratios to a denominator of 100 to write them as percents!

2. Simplest Form

Write a percent over 100 and divide by the GCF. Always clear decimal numerators before simplifying.

3. Decimal Shift

Decimal to percent: move 2 places right. Percent to decimal: move 2 places left.

4. Like Form

Rename fractions, decimals, and percents into the same form before comparing their values.
