Multiplying Fractions

Multiplying Fractions

Evaluate the expression by multiplying the fractions. Express your answer in simplest form using a slash (/) for the fraction bar:

(-3/4) * (8/9)

• Multiply the numerators: -3 * 8.

• Multiply the denominators: 4 * 9.

• Divide the numerator and denominator by their Greatest Common Factor (GCF) to express the fraction in simplest form.

Multiplying Mixed Numbers

Multiplying Mixed Numbers

Find the exact product of the mixed number and the integer:

2 1/4 * (-8)

• Rename the mixed number as a fraction greater than 1.

• Write the integer as a fraction with a denominator of 1.

• Multiply the fractions and simplify to an integer.

Lesson 5-10 Warm-Up

Dividing Fractions

Dividing Fractions Word Problem

A runner is training on a trail that is 15/16 of a mile long. She places a small flag every 3/16 of a mile. How many flags does she place in total along the trail?

• Set up a division problem to find how many 3/16 sections are in 15/16.

• Rewrite the division problem as multiplication by using the reciprocal of the divisor.

• Simplify and multiply.
