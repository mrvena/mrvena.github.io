Lesson 8-3 Warm-Up

Meaning of Division by a Fraction

Division by Unit Fraction

How many 1/6s are in 3? Write the quotient for 3 ÷ (1/6).

3 ÷ (1/6) = ?

• Think: How many equal 1/6 parts fit into 1 whole? There are 6.

• For 3 wholes, multiply: 3 x 6 = 18.

• So, 3 ÷ (1/6) = 18.

Lesson 8-3 Warm-Up

Fraction Division Word Problem

Whole Number ÷ Fraction

Oliver has a wooden board that is 10 feet long. He cuts the board into smaller sections that are each 5/7 foot long. How many sections can Oliver make?

• Divide the total length by the section length: 10 ÷ (5/7).

• Multiply by the reciprocal: 10 x (7/5).

• (10 x 7) / 5 = 70 / 5 = 14 sections.

Lesson 8-4 Warm-Up

Model Dividing Fractions

Fraction ÷ Fraction

Sofia has 8/9 cup of lemonade mix. Each glass of lemonade requires 2/27 cup of mix. How many glasses of lemonade can Sofia make?

• Divide the total amount of mix by the amount per glass: (8/9) ÷ (2/27).

• Multiply by the reciprocal of the divisor: (8/9) x (27/2).

• Simplify: (8 x 27) / (9 x 2) = 216 / 18 = 12 glasses.
