School Field Trips

Buses hold 52 passengers. For at least 235 students and teachers, we solve 52b >= 235 to order at least 5 buses!

With a budget under 800 dollars, a set-up fee of 350 dollars, and 12.50 dollars/guest, solve 12.50g + 350 < 800 to invite max 35 guests!

Fuel Economy

A car averages 23 mpg. Driving no more than 500 miles requires solving 23g <= 500 to buy no more than 21.74 gallons!

Key Vocabulary Terms

inequality: A mathematical sentence that compares two expressions using <, >, <=, or >=.
solution set: The set of all values that make an inequality statement true.
reverse symbol rule: Reversing < to > (or <= to >=) when multiplying or dividing both sides by a negative number.
Division Property: Dividing both sides of an inequality by the same non-zero number to solve it.
Multiplication Property: Multiplying both sides of an inequality by the same non-zero number.
multistep inequality: An inequality that requires two or more inverse operations to solve.
Section 2 • 5 Minutes Direct Instruction

Objective 1: Solve Inequalities Using Multiplication or Division (Lesson 3-3)

Example: -7.30n > 365 -> n < -50
Step 1: Identify the Operation & Coefficient

In -7.30n > 365, the variable n is multiplied by -7.30.

Step 2: Apply the Inverse Operation

Divide both sides by -7.30:
(-7.30n) / (-7.30) ? 365 / (-7.30).

Step 3: CRITICAL RULE - Reverse the Inequality Sign!

Because you divided by a negative number (-7.30), flip > to <:
n < -50.

Multiplication & Division Rule Summary
1. Multiplying / Dividing by POSITIVE Numbers: The inequality symbol STAYS THE SAME.
Example: 52b >= 235 divided by 52 -> b >= 4.52.
2. Multiplying / Dividing by NEGATIVE Numbers: The inequality symbol MUST BE REVERSED!
Example: -7.30n > 365 divided by -7.30 -> n < -50.
Solution Set: All numbers less than -50

Error to Avoid: Forgetting to Reverse the Sign!

Always check whether the number you are dividing or multiplying by is negative! If it is negative, you MUST reverse the inequality symbol immediately.

Section 4 • 5 Minutes Direct Instruction

Objective 2: Solve Multistep Inequalities (Lesson 3-4)

Example: 12.50g + 350 < 800 -> g < 36
Step 1: Undo Addition or Subtraction First

Subtract the set-up fee 350 from both sides:
12.50g + 350 - 350 < 800 - 350
12.50g < 450

Step 2: Undo Multiplication or Division

Divide both sides by 12.50:
(12.50g) / 12.50 < 450 / 12.50
g < 36

Step 3: Interpret Solution in Context

Since g < 36, Sally can invite any whole number of guests less than 36. The maximum number of guests is 35.

Multistep Solving Breakdown
12.50g + 350 < 800
Subtract 350: 12.50g < 450
Divide 12.50: g < 36
Max Whole Guests: 35

Error to Avoid: Performing Operations in the Wrong Order!

Always undo addition/subtraction BEFORE undoing multiplication/division! Also, if you divide by a negative coefficient at the final step, don't forget to reverse the inequality symbol!

Section 6

Key Takeaways & Turn In

1. Negative Rule

When multiplying or dividing both sides by a negative number, ALWAYS flip the inequality symbol!

2. Inverse Operations

In multistep inequalities, undo addition/subtraction first, then undo multiplication/division.

3. Solution Sets

Check solutions by plugging numbers back into the original inequality statement.

4. Contextual Sense

Round up or down appropriately when solving discrete word problems (buses, guests, tickets).
