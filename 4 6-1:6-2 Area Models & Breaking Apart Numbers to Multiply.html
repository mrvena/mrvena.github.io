Key Vocabulary Terms

area model: A visual grid model showing multiplication as the area of a rectangle broken into parts.
product: The answer or total result obtained when numbers are multiplied together.
unit square: A square with side lengths of 1 unit used to measure total area.
factor: A number that is multiplied by another number to get a product.
partial product: The product obtained when multiplying one part of a broken-apart factor.
multiply: Adding equal groups repeatedly or calculating total area.
Section 2 • 5 Minutes Direct Instruction

Objective 1: Use Area Models to Multiply (Lesson 6-1)

Example: 14 x 16 = 224
Step 1: Break Apart Factors into Tens and Ones

Break apart the rectangle dimensions into tens and ones:
14 = 10 + 4 and 16 = 10 + 6.

Step 2: Find the Area of Each Section

• Top Left: 10 x 10 = 100
• Top Right: 10 x 6 = 60
• Bottom Left: 4 x 10 = 40
• Bottom Right: 4 x 6 = 24

Step 3: Add All 4 Partial Products Together

add the unit squares in each section:
100 + 60 + 40 + 24 = 224 total unit squares.

Error to Avoid: Forgetting Any Section of the Area Model!

Always make sure you multiply all 4 sections of the grid (Tens x Tens, Tens x Ones, Ones x Tens, and Ones x Ones) before adding them together!

Objective 2: Break Apart Numbers to Multiply (Lesson 6-2)

Example: 78 x 27 = 2,106
Step 1: Break Each Factor into Tens and Ones

Write 78 x 27 as (70 + 8) x (20 + 7).

1
First Numbers:

Multiply tens by tens: 70 x 20 = 1,400

2
Outer Numbers:

Multiply tens by ones: 70 x 7 = 490

3
Inner Numbers:

Multiply ones by tens: 8 x 20 = 160

4
Last Numbers:

Multiply ones by ones: 8 x 7 = 56

Step 2: Add All Partial Products

1,400 + 490 + 160 + 56 = 2,106 square feet.

Break Apart Multiplication Summary
(70 + 8) x (20 + 7)
First: 70 x 20 = 1,400
Outer: 70 x 7 = 490
Inner: 8 x 20 = 160
Last: 8 x 7 = 56
Total Product: 2,106

Giovanni calculated 54 x 32 as (50 x 30) + (50 x 2) + (4 x 3) + (4 x 2). Notice his mistake? He wrote 4 x 3 instead of 4 x 30! Always remember that the 3 in 32 stands for 30!

Key Takeaways & Turn In

1. Area Models

Break rectangles into tens and ones, find partial areas, then add all sections!

2. Break Apart

Split factors by place value: (Tens + Ones) x (Tens + Ones).

3. Partial Products

Multiply First, Outer, Inner, and Last values carefully.

4. Combine Sums

Add all 4 partial products to reach your exact final answer.
