Why We Need This Math & Key Vocabulary

Wrapping Gifts

If you have a roll of wrapping paper that is a certain number of feet long, evaluating expressions helps you know how many equal pieces you can cut!

Charity Donations

When you know you donated a fraction of your savings, you can use division equations to figure out exactly how much you originally earned.

Restaurant Orders

Chefs use fraction equations to determine how many sandwiches they can make if each takes a fraction of a pound of ingredients.

Fitness Tracking

Tracking workout times involving fractions of an hour lets you calculate total calories burned over complex routines.

Key Vocabulary Terms

evaluate: To find the value of a numerical or algebraic expression by substituting a number for a variable.
expression: A mathematical phrase that contains operations, numbers, and/or variables but no equal sign.
equation: A mathematical sentence showing that two expressions are equal.
Division Property of Equality: If you divide both sides of an equation by the same non-zero number, the sides remain equal.
Multiplication Property of Equality: If you multiply both sides of an equation by the same number, the sides remain equal.
reciprocal: Two numbers whose product is 1. (e.g., 3/4 and 4/3). Used heavily when dividing fractions.
Section 2 • 5 Minutes Direct Instruction

Objective 1: Evaluate Expressions with Fractions (Lesson 8-10)

Example Problem: w ÷ 2/3
Example Problem Statement:

Evaluate the expression w ÷ 2/3 when w = 8.

Step 1: Substitute

Replace the variable with the given value.
w ÷ 2/3 becomes 8 ÷ 2/3.

Step 2: Multiply by Reciprocal

Rename 8 as a fraction: 8/1.
Multiply by the reciprocal of the divisor:
8/1 × 3/2

Step 3: Simplify and Solve

Multiply numerators, then denominators:
(8 × 3) / (1 × 2) = 24 / 2 = 12.

Evaluating Expressions
Original 8 ÷ 2/3
Reciprocal 8 × 3/2
Product 24 / 2
Final Answer: 12

Error to Avoid: Forgetting to Flip!

When dividing by a fraction, remember you must multiply by its reciprocal. Do not multiply straight across without flipping the second fraction first!

Section 4 • 5 Minutes Direct Instruction

Objective 2: Solve Equations with Fractions (Lesson 8-11)

Example Problem: 1/4 x = 25
Example Problem Statement:

Solve the equation 1/4 x = 25 for x.

Rule 1: Isolate the Variable

To get x by itself, you need to divide both sides by the fraction connected to x (Division Property of Equality).
Example: Divide both sides by 1/4.

Rule 2: Multiply by Reciprocal

Dividing by 1/4 is the same as multiplying by its reciprocal, which is 4/1 or simply 4.
Example: x = 25 × 4
x = 100

Equation Solving Properties
Isolating variables
Start: 1/4 x = 25
Action: Multiply both sides by 4/1
Result: x = 100
Check: 1/4(100) = 25

Error to Avoid: Not Checking Answers!

Always substitute your final answer back into the original equation to see if the left side equals the right side!

Section 6

Key Takeaways & Save / Turn In

1. Substitution

To evaluate an expression, always replace the variable with the given value first!

2. Reciprocals

Dividing by a fraction is the same as multiplying by its reciprocal.

3. Equations

Keep equations balanced! Whatever you do to one side, you must do to the other side.

4. Check Answers

Substitute your final solution back into the original equation to ensure the sides equal out.
