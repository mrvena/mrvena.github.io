Key Vocabulary Terms

pattern: An ordered set of numbers that follow a predictable rule.
multiply: Combining equal groups to find a total product.
multiple: The product of a given number and any whole number (e.g. 10, 20, 30).
factor: A number that is multiplied by another number to get a product.
product: The answer to a multiplication problem.
zero rule: For every zero at the end of a factor, write a zero at the end of the product.
Section 2 • Direct Instruction

Objective: Multiply Single Digits by Multiples of 10, 100, or 1,000

Core Rule: 8 x 3 = 24
Pattern 1: Multiply by Multiples of 10

Find 8 x 30:
8 x 3 tens = 24 tens = 240 (1 zero in factors = 1 zero in product).

Pattern 2: Multiply by Multiples of 100

Find 8 x 300:
8 x 3 hundreds = 24 hundreds = 2,400 (2 zeros in factors = 2 zeros in product).

Pattern 3: Multiply by Multiples of 1,000

Find 8 x 3,000:
8 x 3 thousands = 24 thousands = 24,000 (3 zeros in factors = 3 zeros in product).

Robo-Math Rule

"Multiply the non-zero digits first. Count the total number of zeros at the end of the factors. Then write that exact same number of zeros at the end of the product!"

8 x 3,000 = 24,000

Quick Tip: Count Zeros Carefully!

Always solve the basic multiplication fact first (e.g. 8 x 3 = 24), then annex all ending zeros from the factors onto your answer.

Section 4 • Direct Instruction

Objective: Multiply Multiples of 10, 100, or 1,000 by Multiples of 10

Example: 500 x 80 = 40,000
Step 1: Multiply Non-Zero Digits

Identify the basic fact: For 500 x 80, multiply 5 x 8 = 40.

Step 2: Count ALL Factor Zeros

500 has 2 zeros and 80 has 1 zero. Total = 3 zeros in the factors.

Step 3: Annex Zeros to Product

Attach 3 zeros to 40: 40,000.

Multiplication Patterns in Action
20 x 40 2 x 4 = 8 + 2 zeros = 800
20 x 400 2 x 4 = 8 + 3 zeros = 8,000
500 x 80 5 x 8 = 40 + 3 zeros = 40,000
6,000 x 70 6 x 7 = 42 + 4 zeros = 420,000

Watch Out for Facts That End in Zero!

When multiplying 5 x 8, the product is 40 (which already has a zero). Make sure to still add all 3 extra zeros from the factors to get 40,000!

Problem 11 (Word Problem) Inventory

A store has 190 boxes of notebooks with 20 notebooks in each box. It also has 115 boxes with 10 notebooks in each box. How many notebooks in all?

First Group (190 x 20):
Second Group (115 x 10):
Total Notebooks:

• Group 1: 190 x 20 = 3,800.

• Group 2: 115 x 10 = 1,150.

• Sum: 3,800 + 1,150 = 4,950 notebooks.

Section 6

Key Takeaways & Turn In

1. Basic Fact First

Multiply the non-zero digits first to establish your primary answer base.

2. Count All Zeros

Count the total number of zeros at the end of both factor numbers.

3. Annex to Product

Write the same count of zeros onto the end of your basic fact product.

4. Verify Place Value

Check facts that end in zero (like 5 x 8 = 40) so you don't miss a zero!
