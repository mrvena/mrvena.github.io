Today's Learning Objective

I can find the distance between two points with the same x- or y-coordinate, and I can use vertices to draw polygons and find their side lengths on a coordinate plane.

Section 1

Why We Need This Math & Key Vocabulary

Map Reading

Example: A map uses a grid where each unit represents 1 mile. By checking coordinates, you can easily calculate the exact distance between two cities.

Architecture & Fencing

Example: Builders map out structures on a coordinate plane to know exactly how long a fence or wall needs to be for a garden or room.

Video Game Design

Example: Programmers move characters on a screen using coordinate grids. Calculating distances helps the game know when a player reaches a goal.

Scavenger Hunts

Example: Finding distance on a grid helps participants track how far they need to walk to reach the next checkpoint safely and quickly.

Key Vocabulary Terms

x-coordinate: The first number in an ordered pair, representing the horizontal position on a grid.
y-coordinate: The second number in an ordered pair, representing the vertical position on a grid.
absolute value: The distance of a number from zero on a number line, which is always positive.
polygon: A closed shape made of line segments (straight sides) that meet at vertices.
line segment: A straight path connecting two specific points on the coordinate plane.
vertex (vertices): The points where two line segments meet to form an angle in a polygon.
Section 2 • 5 Minutes Direct Instruction

Objective 1: Distance on the Coordinate Plane

Example Problem Statement:

Problem: The points (-1, 8) and (6, 8) are on a coordinate plane. Find the distance between them.

Step 1: Check the Coordinates

The points are (-1, 8) and (6, 8). Both points share the exact same y-coordinate (8). This means we need to find the horizontal distance between the x-coordinates: -1 and 6.

Step 2: Use Absolute Value

Because -1 and 6 have different signs, they are on opposite sides of the y-axis. We add their absolute values together.

|-1| + |6| = 1 + 6 = 7.
The distance between the points is 7 units.

Distance Rule Reminder
Same Signs
If the coordinates have the same sign, subtract their absolute values.
Different Signs
If the coordinates have different signs, add their absolute values.

Error to Avoid: Forgetting Absolute Value!

Distance is a measure of length, so it can never be negative. Always apply absolute value to the coordinates before adding or subtracting them!

Section 4 • 5 Minutes Direct Instruction

Objective 2: Polygons on the Coordinate Plane

Example Problem Statement:

Problem: A polygon has vertices (-5, 3), (15, 3), (15, -10), and (-5, -10). What is the length of its horizontal side?

Step 1: Identify the Horizontal Line Segment

A horizontal side connects two vertices that have the same y-coordinate. Looking at the points, (-5, 3) and (15, 3) share the y-coordinate of 3.

Step 2: Find the Distance

• Compare the x-coordinates: -5 and 15.
• They have different signs, so we add their absolute values.
• |-5| + |15| = 5 + 15 = 20.
The length of the horizontal side is 20 units.

Finding Polygon Sides
Horizontal Sides (Left to Right)
Look for two vertices with the same y-coordinate. Calculate the distance between their x-coordinates.

Vertical Sides (Up and Down)
Look for two vertices with the same x-coordinate. Calculate the distance between their y-coordinates.

Error to Avoid: Mixing Up Horizontal and Vertical!

Don't get confused! The x-axis is horizontal, but a horizontal side shares the same y-coordinate. The y-axis is vertical, but a vertical side shares the same x-coordinate.

Section 6

Key Takeaways & Save / Turn In

1. Coordinate Signs

Before finding the distance, always check if the two coordinates you are comparing are on the same side or opposite sides of zero.

2. Add or Subtract?

If the coordinates have the same sign, subtract their absolute values. If they have different signs, add their absolute values.

3. Polygons

Polygons on a coordinate plane are formed by connecting vertices with straight line segments.

4. Side Lengths

Find the length of horizontal sides using the x-coordinates, and vertical sides using the y-coordinates.
