Why We Need This Math & Key Vocabulary

Recipe Sizing

If a catering recipe yields food in 4 1/2 pound batches, dividing fractions helps you find out how many exact servings you can dish out!

Construction

A carpenter needs to cut a 10 3/4 foot board into perfectly equal 2 1/2 foot sections. Dividing mixed numbers gives the exact quantity.

Landscaping

Calculating garden areas with complex measurements uses the order of operations to accurately subtract, square, and add fractional dimensions.

Data & Science

Scientists evaluate formulas by substituting fractional values for variables. Without following the exact Order of Operations, formulas fail!

Key Vocabulary Terms

whole number: A number without fractions or decimals (e.g., 0, 1, 2, 3...).
mixed number: A number consisting of a whole number and a proper fraction (e.g., 4 1/2).
quotient: The result obtained by dividing one quantity by another.
improper fraction: A fraction where the numerator is greater than or equal to the denominator (e.g., 5/2).
order of operations: The rules determining the sequence in which operations should be performed (PEMDAS).
evaluate: To substitute a given number for each variable, and then calculate the result.
Section 2 • 5 Minutes Direct Instruction

Objective 1: Divide with Whole and Mixed Numbers (Lesson 8-7)

Example Problem: 15 ÷ 2 1/2
Example Problem Statement:

Find the quotient of 15 ÷ 2 1/2.

Step 1: Convert to Improper Fractions

Rename the whole number as a fraction with a denominator of 1, and the mixed number as an improper fraction.
• 15 becomes 15/1.
• 2 1/2 becomes 5/2.
The problem is now: 15/1 ÷ 5/2

Step 2: Multiply by Reciprocal

Change division to multiplication and flip the divisor (the second fraction).
• 15/1 × 2/5
• Multiply numerators: 15 × 2 = 30
• Multiply denominators: 1 × 5 = 5
• Simplify: 30 / 5 = 6.

Dividing Mixed Numbers
Rewrite 15/1 ÷ 5/2
→
Reciprocal 15/1 × 2/5
Calculation & Simplification: 30 / 5 = 6

Error to Avoid: Flipping the Wrong Fraction!

Always find the reciprocal of the divisor (the second number in the expression). Never flip the first fraction!

Section 4 • 5 Minutes Direct Instruction

Objective 2: Order of Operations with Fractions (Lesson 8-8)

Example Problem: (5 1/3 - 2 1/3)2 + 1 1/2
Example Problem Statement:

Simplify the expression (5 1/3 - 2 1/3)2 + 1 1/2 using the Order of Operations.

Step 1: Grouping Symbols (Parentheses)

First, perform any math inside grouping symbols like parentheses.
5 1/3 - 2 1/3 = 3.
The expression becomes: (3)2 + 1 1/2

Step 2: Exponents & Final Addition

Next, evaluate any powers (exponents).
(3)2 = 3 × 3 = 9.
Finally, add the remaining terms from left to right.
9 + 1 1/2 = 10 1/2.

Order of Operations Reference
PEMDAS with Fractions
1. Parentheses / Grouping Symbols
2. Exponents
3. Multiply or Divide (Left to Right)
4. Add or Subtract (Left to Right)

Error to Avoid: Jumping Straight to Addition!

Always perform multiplication or division before you add or subtract, even if the fraction addition looks "easier"!

Section 6

Key Takeaways & Save / Turn In

1. Improper First

Always convert whole numbers and mixed numbers into improper fractions before you multiply or divide!

2. Reciprocals

To divide fractions, change the operation to multiplication and multiply by the reciprocal of the divisor.

3. PEMDAS

Remember Parentheses, Exponents, Multiplication/Division, and Addition/Subtraction!

4. Evaluating

When you evaluate an expression, substitute the exact fractional value for the variable and follow the order of operations.
