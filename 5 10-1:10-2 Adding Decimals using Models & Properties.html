Key Vocabulary Terms

decimal: A number that uses a decimal point to show tenths, hundredths, or thousandths.
tenth: One part out of 10 equal parts of a whole (0.1).
hundredth: One part out of 100 equal parts of a whole (0.01).
place value: The value of a digit based on its position in a number.
Commutative Property: Changing the order of addends does not change the sum (a + b = b + a).
Associative Property: Changing the grouping of addends does not change the sum (a + (b + c) = (a + b) + c).
Section 2 • 5 Minutes Instruction

Objective 1: Use Base-Ten Models to Add Decimals

Example: 1.26 + 0.95 = 2.21
Base-Ten Model Key

• Flat Block = 1 Whole (1.0)  |  • Rod Block = 1 tenth (0.1)  |  • Small Cube = 1 hundredth (0.01)

Step 1: Represent Both Addends

Model 1.26 (1 flat, 2 rods, 6 cubes) and 0.95 (0 flats, 9 rods, 5 cubes).

Step 2: Combine Hundredths & Regroup

6 cubes + 5 cubes = 11 hundredths.
Regroup 10 hundredths as 1 tenth (rod), leaving 1 hundredth (cube) in the hundredths column.

Step 3: Combine Tenths & Regroup

1 regrouped rod + 2 rods + 9 rods = 12 tenths.
Regroup 10 tenths as 1 whole (flat), leaving 2 tenths (rods) in the tenths column.

Step 4: Count Total Sum

1 regrouped flat + 1 flat = 2 wholes.
Final Total = 2 wholes + 2 tenths + 1 hundredth = 2.21.

Error to Avoid: Forgetting to Regroup 10s!

Whenever you collect 10 hundredths (cubes), you MUST trade them for 1 tenth (rod)! Whenever you collect 10 tenths (rods), trade them for 1 whole (flat).

Objective 2: Use Properties & Strategies to Add Decimals

Example: 4.24 + 4.34 = 8.58
Method 1: Expanded Form & Properties

Write numbers in expanded form:
4.24 + 4.34 = (4 + 0.2 + 0.04) + (4 + 0.3 + 0.04)

1
Commutative Property (Reorder Addends)

Group like place values together:
(4 + 4) + (0.2 + 0.3) + (0.04 + 0.04)

2
Associative Property (Group & Simplify)

Add inside each set of parentheses:
8 + 0.5 + 0.08 = 8.58

Method 2: Place Value Partial Sums

• Add ones: 4 + 4 = 8
• Add tenths: 0.2 + 0.3 = 0.5
• Add hundredths: 0.04 + 0.04 = 0.08
• Combine partial sums: 8 + 0.5 + 0.08 = 8.58

Critical Rule: Align Decimal Points & Match Place Values!

Always line up decimal points vertically before adding! Never add tenths to hundredths or ones to tenths!

Problem 11 (Word Problem) Multi-Lap

A tennis court is 23.77 meters long by 8.23 meters wide. Jenna walks around the perimeter of the court 2 times. How far does she walk?

1 Lap Perimeter (m):
Total Distance (2 Laps):

• Perimeter = 23.77 + 8.23 + 23.77 + 8.23.

• Group 23.77 + 8.23 = 32 meters. Two pairs = 32 + 32 = 64 m (1 lap).

• 2 laps = 64 + 64 = 128 meters.

Section 6

Key Takeaways & Turn In

1. Base-Ten Models

Flats represent wholes (1.0), Rods represent tenths (0.1), and Cubes represent hundredths (0.01).

2. Align Decimals

Always line up decimal points vertically so place values align before adding!

3. Group Smartly

Use Commutative and Associative properties to pair numbers that form whole numbers first.

4. Trade & Regroup 10s

10 hundredths = 1 tenth. 10 tenths = 1 whole. Always trade up when reaching 10!
