Why We Need This Math & Key Vocabulary

Graphic Design

A designer creates a logo that is 1/2 blue. If 3/5 of the blue section must contain a star pattern, multiplying fractions helps determine what part of the whole logo has stars.

Endurance Sports

An athlete runs 2 1/4 miles a day. To find the total distance run over a span of days, they multiply a mixed number by an integer.

Track Events

A running track is divided into 1/10 mile sections. Finding how many sections exist in a 1/2 mile stretch requires dividing fractions!

Finance & Sales

Evaluating a discount of 1/4 off an item priced at $5.00 requires multiplying a fraction by an integer to find the total savings.

Key Vocabulary Terms

simplest form: A fraction where the numerator and denominator have no common factors other than 1.
mixed number: A number consisting of an integer and a proper fraction.
reciprocal: The multiplicative inverse of a number. (e.g., the reciprocal of 2/3 is 3/2). Zero has no reciprocal.
complex fraction: A fraction that has a fraction or mixed number in the numerator, the denominator, or both.
integer: A whole number that can be positive, negative, or zero, but not a fraction.
Section 2 • 5 Minutes Direct Instruction

Objective 1: Multiply Positive & Negative Fractions and Mixed Numbers

Example Problem: -2 1/4 * 3/5
Example Problem Statement:

Find the product of the mixed number -2 1/4 and the fraction 3/5.

Step 1: Rename Mixed Numbers

First, rename any mixed numbers as a fraction greater than 1 (an improper fraction).
• -2 1/4 becomes -9/4.

Step 2: Multiply & Simplify

Multiply the numerators together and the denominators together. Apply integer sign rules!
• (-9/4) * (3/5)
• Numerators: -9 * 3 = -27
• Denominators: 4 * 5 = 20
• Result: -27/20, which renames to -1 7/20.

Multiplication Rules Visualizer
-9 / 4
X
3 / 5
Calculation: (-9 * 3) / (4 * 5) = -27/20

Error to Avoid: Forgetting the Signs!

When multiplying, remember: if the signs are the same, the product is positive. If the signs are different, the product is negative!

Section 4 • 5 Minutes Direct Instruction

Objective 2: Divide Positive & Negative Fractions and Complex Fractions

Example Problem: -5/9 ÷ 2/3
Example Problem Statement:

Find the quotient of the fraction -5/9 divided by the fraction 2/3.

Rule 1: Multiply by the Reciprocal

To divide fractions, multiply the dividend by the reciprocal (multiplicative inverse) of the divisor.
Example: The reciprocal of 2/3 is 3/2. The problem becomes -5/9 * 3/2.

Rule 2: Complex Fractions

A complex fraction is just division written vertically! Simply rewrite it as a standard division problem across the page, and then multiply by the reciprocal.

Division Steps
-5/9 ÷ 2/3
1. Find Reciprocal: 2/3 → 3/2
2. Rewrite: -5/9 * 3/2
3. Multiply: (-5 * 3) / (9 * 2) = -15/18
Simplified: -5/6

Error to Avoid: Flipping the Wrong Fraction!

Always flip the DIVISOR (the second fraction), never the dividend (the first fraction). Zero has no reciprocal!

Section 6

Key Takeaways & Save / Turn In

1. Multiply Fractions

Multiply numerators straight across, and multiply denominators straight across. Simplify using the GCF!

2. Mixed Numbers

Always rename mixed numbers to fractions greater than 1 (improper fractions) before multiplying or dividing.

3. Reciprocals

To divide fractions, multiply the dividend (first number) by the reciprocal of the divisor (second number).

4. Integer Rules Apply

Remember the integer rules! Same signs equal a positive answer. Different signs equal a negative answer.
